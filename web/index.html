T-1000 LAB BENCH

Exact small-scale simulation, probability, number theory and geometry. Each panel says where its numbers come from. host: exact simulation board: checking recorded results (archive)

Algorithms

Source: exact state vectors in numpy (web/lab_api.py); results are checked against closed forms in web/test_lab_api.py.

Circuit composer

Source: host: exact state vector (/api/run_circuit). Board: the circuit is compiled and executed on the Basys 3 and the state is read back (web/qpu_board.py); if the board is absent the host runs it and says so.

One gate per line, in order: H q, X q, Z q, S q, T q, RX q angle, RY q angle, RZ q angle, CX control target, CZ control target, SWAP a b. Qubit 0 is the lowest bit of the basis index.

Born rule, shots and chi-square

Source: multinomial sampling from the exact distribution (numpy, seeded). The p-value is the chi-square survival function, checked against tables.

If the model is right, the p-values of repeated experiments are uniform on [0,1] (5 % fall below 0.05). Try shots against a distribution you altered in the custom list: the p-values pile up near 0.

Random circuits and the Porter-Thomas law

Source: exact state vector with Haar-random single-qubit layers and a CZ brickwork (numpy). Compares the output probabilities with the exponential law.

Walks on graphs: quantum against classical

Source: exact spectral evolution (numpy): quantum exp(-iAt), classical continuous-time exp(-(D-A)t). Probability is conserved to 1e-9 in both (shown below).

Toolbox

Source: exact integer arithmetic (BigInt) in web/lab_nt.js, tested in web/test_lab_nt.js. Primality is deterministic below 3.3e24.

Digit-root explorer

Source: exact (BigInt). The digital root of n in base b is 1 + (n-1) mod (b-1), so only the residue of n mod (b-1) matters.

Orbits of x to k*x + c (mod m): the times-table circle

Source: exact. Cycles come from the functional graph of the map; the quantum buttons call the lab API.

Claim checker

Source: exact computation on the stated range; the statement is checked, not assumed. Add your own claims in web/lab.js.

Bloch sphere and the Hopf fibration

Source: exact (web/lab_geom.js, tested). Each point of the Bloch sphere (S2) has a circle of states above it in S3 (the global phase). Fibres over different points are linked once; the test file checks that numerically.

Drag the right-hand view to rotate it. The highlighted fibre lies over the state you set on the left.

Regular 4-polytopes

Source: exact coordinates; edges are all pairs at the minimum distance; counts are checked against the known values in web/test_lab_geom.js.
